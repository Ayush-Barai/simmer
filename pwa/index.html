Garlic Butter Pasta

  • 200 g pasta
  • 4 cloves garlic
  • 2 tbsp butter
  1. Boil the pasta until al dente.
  2. Sizzle the garlic in butter.
  3. Toss together and serve.
“Add the garlic now.” “Lower the heat a little.” “Two minutes to go.”
